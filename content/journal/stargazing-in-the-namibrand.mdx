Turn off the lights. Wait twenty minutes for your eyes to adjust. Then look up.

In the NamibRand Nature Reserve, on a clear moonless night, there are so many stars that the familiar constellations are hard to find. The Milky Way stretches from horizon to horizon, with dark lanes of dust running through it. Some nights it's bright enough to cast a faint shadow.

## Why it's so dark

The NamibRand is a large private reserve on the edge of the Namib, a long way from any town. The air is dry. And the reserve looks after its darkness: lodges use low, shielded, warm lighting, and it has been recognised as an International Dark Sky Reserve by DarkSky International. *(Verify current details before quoting.)*

## Plan around the moon

The moon matters more than anything else. A full moon lights the landscape beautifully, but it washes out all but the brightest stars.

For the darkest skies, travel in the week either side of the **new moon**. If you can't, choose nights when the moon sets early or rises late. Our [moon planner](/night-sky#moon) shows the phase for any date and marks the darkest nights of the month.

## Plan around the season

- **May to August:** cold, clear and dry. The core of the Milky Way is high overhead in the evening. Bring a down jacket.
- **September to November:** warmer nights, the Magellanic Clouds climbing in the south.
- **December to March:** warm, with more cloud in rainy months. Orion and Sirius in the evening.

## What to look for

- **The Southern Cross**, with the two bright Pointers beside it.
- **The Magellanic Clouds:** two small galaxies, visible as soft patches without any optics.
- **Omega Centauri**, a huge ball of stars that looks like a fuzzy star to the eye and is extraordinary in binoculars.
- **The Coalsack**, a dark cloud of dust beside the Cross.

## Photographing it

A tripod, a wide lens at its widest aperture, and exposures of around 15–25 seconds will show more than your eyes can see. Keep a camelthorn or dune crest in the foreground for scale. Use a red light so you don't ruin everyone's night vision, including your own.

## Sleep out

Many NamibRand camps offer a night on a bedroll under the open sky. Do it at least once. Wake at 3am, look up, and you'll understand why we built a company around this.
